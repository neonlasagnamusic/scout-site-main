Fundraising

Pack 541's fundraisers support campouts, badges, and pack equipment — keeping costs manageable for every family.

Our Fundraisers

Over the years we've run a variety of fundraisers. Every dollar raised goes directly back into pack programming.

🍿

Digital Popcorn Fundraiser

Our annual online popcorn sale through Trail's End. Scouts share a personal storefront link with family and friends to place orders directly — no door-to-door required.

🏡

Community Garage Sale

A pack-organized community garage sale where families donate items and proceeds go directly to pack programming. A great way to declutter and support Scouting at the same time.

🍫

See's Candy Fundraiser

Seasonal candy sales featuring See's Candies products — a favorite around the holidays. Scouts take orders from neighbors, family, and coworkers and deliver when the order arrives.
