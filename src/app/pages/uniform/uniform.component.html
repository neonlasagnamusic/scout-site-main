Scout Uniforms

A guide to what each rank wears and where everything goes.

Why the Uniform Matters

The Scout uniform is more than just clothing — it represents membership in a worldwide movement and creates a sense of belonging and pride. Wearing the uniform correctly to meetings and events is part of being a Scout.

Uniforms are available at your local Scout Shop or at scoutshop.org. Ask your den leader if you have questions about patches or exact placement.

General Patch Placement Tips

Left Sleeve

  • Council shoulder patch at the top
  • Pack numeral (541) below council patch
  • Den numeral below pack numeral
  • National Honor Patrol (if earned) below den numeral

Right Sleeve

  • American flag patch at the top
  • Earned activity patches below the flag

Left Pocket

  • Current rank badge centered on the pocket flap

Right Pocket

  • Religious emblem (if earned)
  • World Friendship patch (if earned)

When in doubt, bring the shirt to a den meeting — leaders are happy to help with patch placement.
