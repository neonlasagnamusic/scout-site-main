Training

All registered leaders are encouraged (and some are required) to complete BSA training to ensure a safe, fun, and effective scouting experience.

  • Youth Protection Training (YPT) — required for all registered adults, renewed every 2 years
  • Position-Specific Training — online modules for your specific role
  • BALOO — required for pack-level camping events
  • Outdoor Leader Skills — recommended for den leaders

All training is available online at my.scouting.org. Contact the Cubmaster for assistance.
