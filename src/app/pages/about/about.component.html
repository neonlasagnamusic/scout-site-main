About Pack 541

A volunteer-run Cub Scout pack serving families across Tustin and North Irvine since our founding.

Who We Are

Pack 541 is a Cub Scouts of America pack based in Tustin and North Irvine, California. We serve families from PCE, Ladera, Hicks Canyon, TMA, Orchard Hills, and Tustin Ranch Elementary schools.

We are entirely volunteer-run — every leader, event coordinator, and committee member is a parent who gives their time because they believe in what scouting does for kids. Our simple goal: help Scouts learn and experience new things.

Pack 541 is open to both girls and boys in Kindergarten through 5th grade. No prior scouting experience is needed from parents or Scouts — we will show you everything you need to know.

Schools We Serve

Pack 541 draws its members from the following Tustin and North Irvine-area elementary schools:

  • Pioneer Christian Elementary
  • Ladera Elementary
  • Hicks Canyon Elementary
  • Tustin Memorial Academy (TMA)
  • Orchard Hills Elementary
  • Loma Vista Elementary
  • Arroyo Elementary
  • Red Hill Elementary
  • Guin Foss Elementary
  • Tustin Ranch Elementary

Not sure if your school is represented? Email us — all Tustin and Irvine families are welcome to inquire.
