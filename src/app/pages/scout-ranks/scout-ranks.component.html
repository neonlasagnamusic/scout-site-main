Scout Rank Requirements

An overview of every Cub Scout rank and what it takes to earn it.

Cub Scout Ranks

1

Lion

The entry-level rank for Cub Scouts who join upon entering kindergarten (and are at least age 5). Lions join and register with their adult partner as a member of a Lion den.

2

Bobcat

The Bobcat badge is earned prior to all other ranks. If a youth joins Cub Scouting as a Wolf, Bear, or Webelos, the youth must earn the Bobcat badge first before receiving any other award or rank.

3

Tiger Cub

The rank for youths who have completed kindergarten (about 6-year-olds).

4

Wolf

The rank for youths who have completed first grade (about 7-year-olds).

5

Bear

The rank for youths who have completed second grade (about 8-year-olds).

6

Webelos

The rank for youths who have completed third grade (about 9-year-olds).

7

Arrow of Light

For youth who have completed the fourth grade (about 10-year-olds). Earned after the completion of the Webelos badge.
