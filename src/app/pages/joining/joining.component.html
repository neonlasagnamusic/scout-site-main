Recharter

Pack 541 rechartering takes place annually each fall. All returning Scouts must be re-enrolled through the official BSA recharter process. Existing members will receive instructions via email.

  • Recharter window is typically October–December
  • Annual dues cover BSA national membership fees
  • Watch for email from your den leader with instructions
  • Reach out to info@pack541.org if you have questions

Payments

Pack 541 uses a transparent, volunteer-managed payment structure. Dues help cover BSA registration, event materials, and pack activities.

  • Annual BSA membership fee (set by BSA national)
  • Pack dues assessed separately per year
  • Individual event fees for campouts, derbies, etc.
  • Payment methods and links shared at pack meetings

Financial hardship accommodation is available — please reach out confidentially.
