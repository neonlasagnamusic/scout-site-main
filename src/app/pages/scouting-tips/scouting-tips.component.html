Scouting Tips

Resources and guidance to help leaders and scouts get the most out of every meeting and outing.

Games

Games keep meetings energetic and build teamwork. Here are some Scout-friendly favorites.

Scout-O-Rama Relay

Teams race through a series of scouting skill stations — knot tying, first aid, and navigation.

Capture the Flag

A classic outdoor game great for park meetings. Teaches strategy, communication, and teamwork.

Nature Scavenger Hunt

Give Scouts a list of natural items to find. Great for campouts and park outings.

Knot-Tying Contest

Who can tie a square knot fastest? Timed contests make skill practice exciting.

Projects

Hands-on projects build skills and earn adventure loops and pins.

  • Birdhouse building — woodworking fundamentals with a useful result
  • Rain gauge construction — STEM project connecting to weather science
  • Paracord bracelets — knot practice plus a wearable keepsake
  • Community garden bed — combines service and nature education
  • First aid kit assembly — practical prep paired with first aid lessons
